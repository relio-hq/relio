Getting around the canvas ​

How to move quickly around a big model and find the table you want.

Zoom and pan ​

  • Zoom in and out — Ctrl+mouse wheel (around the mouse pointer), or Ctrl+= · Ctrl+- · Ctrl+0 (100%).

  • Move the view — Use whichever you like:

    • Hold Space and drag (release it to return to your tool)
    • Hand tool (H, from 1.0.2) — once picked, dragging even on a table only moves the view, until you press Esc or V
    • Drag with the middle mouse button, or scroll with two fingers on a touchpad

  • More room for the canvas — Double-click a document tab to collapse the right and bottom panels at once; double-click again to bring them back.

Find and highlight ​

  1. Press Ctrl+F to open the find bar over the canvas.
  2. Type part of a table, column, relationship or note name. Every match is highlighted and everything else is dimmed. For Korean names, Hangul initial consonants also work (for example ㅈㅁ finds 주문, "order").
  3. Press Enter or F3 for the next result and Shift+Enter for the previous one. Press Esc to close the bar.

Tip

Hover over a table or relationship line and the connected lines and tables become stronger. Click to select it and everything outside the connection is dimmed, so it stands out even more.
