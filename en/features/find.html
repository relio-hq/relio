Find and highlight ​

The find bar and match modes, Hangul initial-consonant search, hover and selection connection highlights, and highlight settings.

A short how-to is in Getting around the canvas › Find and highlight in Quick start.

Find bar ​

Press Ctrl+F (Edit › Find) to open the find bar over the canvas.

  • It searches table, column, relationship and note names (physical and logical) as you type. Every match is highlighted and everything else is dimmed.
  • Hangul initial consonants also match, which helps with Korean names: typing ㅅㅇㅈ finds "사용자" (user).
  • Turn on the bar's description option ("Also search table and column descriptions") to search description text too.
  • The bar shows the current result number and the total, like "3 / 12", or "No results" when nothing matches. The Table · Column · Other buttons on the bar turn each kind of target on or off.
  • The … button at the right of the bar (Find and replace…, Ctrl+H) opens the find and replace dialog.
  • Closing the bar with Esc or Close clears the highlights.

Moving between results ​

KeyWhat it does
Enter · F3Goes to the next result and flashes it once.
Shift+Enter · Shift+F3Goes to the previous result.
Alt+WChanges the match mode.
EscCloses the bar.

Going past the last result shows "Wrapped to the first result". An Enter pressed while composing Korean text only confirms the character; it does not move to another result.

Match mode ​

Each click on the mode button on the bar (or Alt+W) switches the mode, and the chosen mode is remembered.

ModeMatches whenExample: searching for ID
Contains (default)It appears anywhere in the nameBoth MEMBER_ID and VALID
WordA piece split at _, spaces, symbols or a case change is the sameMEMBER_ID and memberId match; VALID does not
ExactThe whole name is the sameOnly ID

Word and Exact do not use initial-consonant search.

Finding in a category view ​

In a category tab, only results within that scope are highlighted. If there are matches outside it, "Outside this category: n" appears, and Show in all categories searches again in the "All" tab. Matches on things that are not drawn on the canvas, such as categories hidden from the tabs, are counted too. Categories

Connection highlight ​

  • Hover — Hover over a table, relationship line or column, and the connected lines and tables, and the matching primary key (PK) and foreign key (FK) columns, become slightly stronger. The rest stays as it is.
  • Selection — Click to select, and only the connected objects stay sharp while the rest is dimmed. Selecting a category area shows only its selection border.
  • Tooltips — Rest the mouse on a table or column to see its type, NOT NULL, FK target and description.
  • Clicking an item in the problem list goes to that object and highlights it briefly.

Highlights exist only on screen; they never appear in image or HTML exports or in print.

Highlight settings ​

Change them in Tools › Settings › General › Highlight. Changes apply immediately; check them with Highlight preview.

SettingValues
Highlight connections on hoverOn · Off
Highlight connections of selectionOn · Off
Dim the restOn · Off separately for When finding and On selection
Dim levelLight · Medium · Strong
Highlight effectBlink · Shrink slowly · Fixed, plus Times and Duration of one blink
Reduce motionFollow system setting by default. When on, nothing blinks and only a thick border is shown.

The highlight effect moves only for the current find result and the object you clicked in a list, and motion always ends within 3 seconds.

Tip

If the screen dimming all the time bothers you, turn off only On selection under "Dim the rest". Dimming while finding stays, so results are still easy to spot in a big model.
